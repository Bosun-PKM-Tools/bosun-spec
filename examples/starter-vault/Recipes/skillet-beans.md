# Skillet navy beans

Pantry beans for a weeknight bowl. Linked from [[quickstart]] and tracked under [[project-alpha]].

## Ingredients

- 2 cups navy beans, cooked or canned, drained
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 medium yellow onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 3/4 tsp kosher salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp apple cider vinegar

## Culinary Method

1. Warm 2 tbsp olive oil in a 10-inch skillet over medium heat.
2. Cook the diced onion until translucent, about 6 minutes.
3. Stir in garlic and smoked paprika for 30 seconds.
4. Add beans, salt, and pepper. Cook 12 to 15 minutes, stirring so the beans pick up fond.
5. Finish with vinegar. Taste and adjust salt.

## Notes & Modifications

- Canned beans: rinse once; skip extra salt until the end.
- Add a handful of chopped parsley or a fried egg if you want a fuller plate.
